Sula — Finnish for melted, thawed, unfrozen.

Cold skin.
Warm wood.
Water on stone.
The day leaves quietly.

Built from Pacific wood and shaped by Finnish tradition, made for the weather and rituals of coastal life. Less a thing you own than a place you go — a private sanctuary a few steps from the door, and a reason to step outside, slow down and come back different.

Wood. Stone.
Water. Fire. Air.

Every Sula is built from materials that already live here — cedar that grew in the rain it will stand in, stone shaped by the rivers it will warm beside. Under heat the cedar gives off a faint, sweet scent: the part of the room you can't photograph.
